Liquor vs. liqueur

Liquor
A distilled alcoholic beverage, usually made from grain, at least 40% alcohol (80 proof) but not over 92% (184 proof).
Liqueur
A distilled alcoholic beverage with more sugar than alcohol, or equal parts sugar and alcohol. Sweet and flavored.
Proof
Double the % alcohol by volume: 40% = 80 proof, 50% = 100 proof, 60% = 120 proof.
